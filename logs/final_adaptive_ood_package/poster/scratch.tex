\end{itemize}

\textbf{A movie viewing study - Chen et al. (2016)}\begin{itemize}
    \item When different subjects' mental models (of the current situation) become more and more similar, their brain dynamics got increasingly synchronized. 
    \item We trained recurrent neural networks (RNN) on an environment that mimics the empirical study. We then used computed ISC in the shared space. 
\end{itemize}
